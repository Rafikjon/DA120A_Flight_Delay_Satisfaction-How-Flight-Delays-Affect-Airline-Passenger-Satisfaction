### Introduction Brief
This project focuses on analyzing user activity, historical transaction data, and demographic profiles of customers to uncover hidden behavioral patterns that precede their departure from the service. By examining these factors, the project aims to identify the underlying operational and service-related issues driving customer dissatisfaction.

### Problem Statement
Modern service companies face intense market competition, which makes customer churn a direct threat to operational profitability. Furthermore, acquiring new users is significantly more expensive for a business than retaining its existing customer base. Without a clear understanding of specific behavioral triggers and signs of dissatisfaction, companies cannot intervene in time to prevent customer loss.
